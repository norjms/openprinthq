What carries over, and what to check before cutting
  • Carried over: every shape, its position on the plate, and its operation (cut, score or fill engrave). Plates and materials stay separate. 3D models keep their mesh, textures and size.
  • Not carried over: power, speed and passes. Each machine and laser source is different, so outputs start from the target software's default settings. Pick your material preset before cutting.
  • Kerf: kerf offsets are not converted. Test a joint first.
  • Not converted yet: text objects (convert text to paths first), bitmap images, and Falcon 2D layouts. Anything skipped is listed after you open the file.
  • Licences: models from MakerWorld, Atomm and similar sites keep their designer's licence after conversion. Converting a file for your own machine is not permission to share or sell it.
